# Enclosure Requirements

- Separate protected electronics cavity from external sensing ports.
- Maintain antenna clearance and avoid metal near the ESP32 antenna.
- Do not obstruct BME680 ambient air exchange.
- Provide separate mounting/entry for indoor airflow probe and outdoor anemometer.
- Route leak-probe cables to monitored locations; keep the controller PCB dry.
- Provide strain relief, keyed connectors, mounting bosses and service access.
- Select ingress rating only after the actual test method and intended environment are defined.
- CAD/STEP/STL geometry is not yet created; this document is the design brief.
