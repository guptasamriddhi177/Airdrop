# AirDrop Product Requirements — Commercial-intent concept

Revision: Rev-A engineering prototype

## Product goal
A connected environmental monitoring unit combining indoor air-velocity sensing, outdoor wind measurement, environmental sensing, motion data, water-leak detection and distance channels.

## Scope
All requested sensing channels are included in the architecture. Exact indoor airflow sensor, anemometer, and leak-probe part numbers remain TBD.

## Intended use
Educational and experimental monitoring. Not a certified gas detector, flood prevention system, structural monitoring system, disaster predictor or life-safety device.

## Commercial readiness gates
- Component lifecycle and sourcing review.
- Electrical safety and EMC pre-compliance.
- RF and antenna validation.
- Environmental ingress and corrosion testing.
- Sensor calibration and accuracy characterization.
- Firmware security review and update strategy.
- Reliability, manufacturing test and traceability.
- Applicable regulatory review for intended markets.
