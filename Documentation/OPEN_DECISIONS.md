# Open Decisions Before Design Freeze

1. Exact ESP32-WROOM-32E ordering code; check availability and module antenna variant.
2. Exact BME680 and MPU6050 implementation: onboard IC vs connectorised breakout.
3. Exact indoor air-velocity sensor; range, accuracy, interface, calibration.
4. Exact outdoor anemometer; pulse output type, pulse constant, operating range.
5. Leak probe type: dry contact, open collector, analogue or digital module.
6. Number of leak zones and cable lengths.
7. Power input and battery requirements; regulator and protection topology.
8. Production connector family and enclosure ingress rating.
9. PCB manufacturer stack-up and antenna keep-out requirements.
10. Applicable product compliance requirements for intended market.
