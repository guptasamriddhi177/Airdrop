# Safety, Claims and Limitations

- BME680 gas resistance is not a selective measurement of named toxic gases.
- The product is not a certified gas alarm, smoke alarm, flood alarm, structural monitor or emergency system.
- Outdoor wind speed requires calibration against the selected anemometer's pulse constant.
- Indoor airflow sensor requires calibration and installation guidance.
- Leak status must be Unknown when sensor state or connection is uncertain.
- The experimental risk score must not be advertised as disaster prediction.
- Keep powered PCB and connectors dry. Only sensor elements designed for water contact may be exposed.
- Use a qualified adult/engineer for mains power, fabrication review, soldering/reflow and environmental testing.
